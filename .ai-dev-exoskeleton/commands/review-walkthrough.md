# Review Walkthrough

Walk through a list of issues one by one in a guided, interactive review session.

## Instructions

Look at the issues, findings, or items you just produced above in this conversation. Go through them **one at a time**, starting with the first. For each item:

1. **Summarize the problem** — What exactly is wrong and why it matters.
2. **Propose 2–3 solutions** — Concrete approaches to fix or improve it.
3. **State your preference with reasoning** — Which solution you recommend and why.

Wait for the user to say **NEXT** before moving to the next item. Until then, stay on the current item and discuss it further if the user has questions or feedback.
